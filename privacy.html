Cabinet Scan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-28

Cabinet Scan is a document scanner for iPhone. It is built to keep your documents on your device. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Summary

  • The developer does not collect, store, or receive any of your data. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.
  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Scanning, text recognition, file naming, and summaries run on your device by default. Nothing is uploaded.
  • One optional feature (“Summarize with Claude”) sends the recognized text of a document to Anthropic, and only if you turn it on and supply your own Anthropic API key.

What the app accesses on your device

Camera — used only while you are scanning a document, to capture the pages. The captured images are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere by the app.

Your documents and PDFs — scans, imported PDFs, and the PDFs the app creates are stored locally on your device. The in-app History is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by iOS file protection, and is excluded from device and iCloud backups. You can delete any History entry at any time; deleting the app removes all of it.

Files / folders you choose — if you pick a destination folder (for example a folder inside Google Drive), finished PDFs are copied into it using the standard iOS file system. From that point the copy is governed by whatever service owns that folder and its own privacy policy. Cabinet Scan does not use any Google API and has no access to your Google account.

Optional cloud feature: “Summarize with Claude”

This feature is off by default. If you enable it in Settings and enter your own Anthropic API key:

  • The recognized text of a document you scan or import is sent over HTTPS to Anthropic’s API (api.anthropic.com) to generate a file name and a summary.
  • The page images are never sent — only text.
  • Your API key is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and is used only to authenticate these requests. It is not transmitted anywhere else.
  • Requests are made directly from your device to Anthropic. The developer operates no server and never receives your documents, text, or key.
  • Anthropic’s handling of that text is governed by your agreement with Anthropic and their privacy policy and commercial terms (anthropic.com/legal).

You can turn the feature off, or remove your key, in Settings at any time.

Data the developer collects

None. Cabinet Scan has no backend service. The developer does not receive usage data, crash logs, document contents, or any personal information from the app.

Children

Cabinet Scan is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
