Cabinet Scan — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-03

Cabinet Scan is a document organiser for iPhone. It is built to keep your documents on your device. One optional setting — “Use AI analysis” — sends some of your information to a service the developer runs, so that documents can be summarised, connected into a picture, and asked questions about. This policy explains exactly what is and isn’t sent, and what happens to it.

Summary

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Scanning, text recognition, file naming, and a basic “key facts” card run on your device and never leave it.
  • “Use AI analysis” is off by default. While it is off, nothing about your documents is uploaded, and the LifeGraph, Ask and the “For you” feed are not available.
  • When you turn it on, the developer’s analysis service (hosted on Google Cloud in the European Union) receives, over HTTPS:
    • the recognised text of documents you scan, import, or write as a note — to produce a name, summary and filing area;
    • your LifeGraph, a short index of your documents, and your open to-dos — to answer questions (Ask), power the domain experts, and produce the “For you” review;
    • for Photo Notes (a further opt-in that only appears when AI analysis is on): the photo you capture, so it can be transcribed.
  • The service uses Microsoft Azure OpenAI as the language model and keeps no copy of your documents, graph, answers or photos. It logs only operational metadata (a random per-install ID, which feature ran, and text size) for load and cost, kept up to ~90 days.
  • No advertising, no third-party trackers, no cross-app tracking.

What runs on your device

Camera — used to scan documents, and (if you enable Photo Notes) to photograph a whiteboard or a page of notes. Document scans are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere. Photo Notes captures are sent to the analysis service only when that feature is on and you take a photo in it.

Text recognition — Apple’s on-device Vision OCR (English and German), entirely offline.

Your documents, PDFs and notes — scans, imported PDFs, PDFs the app creates, and hand-written notes (stored as editable text files) live in the app’s storage on your device and, for the History, are protected by iOS file protection and excluded from device and iCloud backups. Delete any entry at any time; deleting the app removes all of it. “Erase everything” in Settings removes every document, the LifeGraph and all to-dos in one step.

Your LifeGraph — the connected picture of people, organisations, addresses, accounts, policies, vehicles and agreements drawn from your documents is assembled and stored on your iPhone. Building or updating it requires “Use AI analysis” (see below); once built it stays on the device.

A folder you choose — if you pick a destination folder (for example one in iCloud Drive or Google Drive), finished PDFs and notes are copied there using the standard iOS file provider. From that point the copy is governed by whoever runs that folder and their own privacy policy. Cabinet Scan uses no Google API and has no access to your Google account.

Optional: “Use AI analysis”

This setting is off by default. Turning it on in Settings enables written summaries, the LifeGraph, Ask, the domain experts, and the “For you” feed, and sends data to Cabinet Scan’s analysis service, which the developer operates on Google Cloud Run in the European Union. Specifically:

FeatureWhat is sent
Naming & summariesthe recognised text of a scanned or imported document, or the text of a note you write. Page images are not sent.
Photo Notesthe photo you capture, downscaled. Only when you take a photo in this mode. This is the one mode where an image leaves the device.
LifeGraphthe recognised text of each new document, plus a label-only list of entities already in your graph, so the same person or company is recognised across documents.
Ask & domain expertsyour LifeGraph (entities, relationships, obligations, events — names, addresses, amounts, dates, reference numbers), a compact index of your documents (name, type, date, area, a short summary), your open to-dos, and your question.
“For you” reviewthe same LifeGraph, document index and to-dos, plus today’s date.

The domain experts additionally run web searches to ground their answers. These searches use general terms only — your name, address, employer, amounts, IBANs and reference numbers are never placed in a search query.

What the service does and does not keep

  • The service has no account and does not persist your documents, OCR text, LifeGraph, answers, or Photo Notes images. It holds your Ask / “For you” context briefly in memory (a small cache, lost when the service restarts) so a follow-up question does not have to re-upload everything.
  • Operational logs. Each request is logged with: a random per-install identifier (a UUID generated on your device — not your name, email, phone number or Apple ID), which endpoint was called, the AI model used, and the number of text tokens processed. No document content, OCR text, images, graph data, questions or answers appear in these logs. They are used to understand load and running cost. Logs are stored on Google Cloud (EU) and deleted after about 90 days.

Sub-processors

  • Google LLC / Google Cloud Platform — hosting (Cloud Run) and the operational logs above, in the European Union.
  • Microsoft Corporation / Azure OpenAI Service — the language model that produces names, summaries, the LifeGraph and answers. Text and images sent to the model are processed to generate the response and, under Azure OpenAI’s terms, are not used to train models.

Legal basis and your rights (GDPR / DSGVO)

For the optional analysis service the developer is the data controller. The legal basis for processing is your consent, given by turning “Use AI analysis” on, and performance of the service you asked for. Processing takes place in the European Union on Google Cloud; the Azure OpenAI region is set by the developer’s Azure resource.

  • Withdraw consent at any time by turning “Use AI analysis” off in Settings. Nothing further is sent.
  • Access / erasure. The service keeps no documents or answers to return or erase. The only record tied to you is the operational log line keyed to your random install identifier; it contains no content and is automatically deleted after ~90 days. Because that identifier is not otherwise linked to your identity, targeted deletion before then may not be possible.
  • Everything on the device is under your control — delete individual entries, or use “Erase everything”.

No tracking

No analytics SDKs, no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no linking of your activity to data from other apps or websites. The per-install identifier described above is used only for first-party load and cost measurement of the optional service.

Children

Cabinet Scan is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
